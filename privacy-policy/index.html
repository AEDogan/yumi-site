Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled by the Yumi desktop application, published on the Microsoft Store as "Yumi: Masaüstü Asistanın" (the "App"), and by the Yumi browser extension (the "Extension"). It is issued by the individual developer Ahmet Ergun Doğan (the "Developer"), including as the information notice required under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK").

1. Data controller

  • Name: Ahmet Ergun Doğan (individual developer)
  • Email: aedogan.mail@gmail.com

The Developer acts as data controller only in the limited cases described in Section 6. Sections 4 and 5 explain how processing works on your device and with the third-party services you choose to use.

2. Summary

  • The Developer does not run any server that collects App data. Your conversations, voice, settings, usage habits and device information are not sent to the Developer.
  • The App contains no telemetry, advertising, analytics or user tracking.
  • By default, speech recognition, speech synthesis and command understanding run locally on your device.
  • Some features connect, at your request, directly from your device to third-party services (Section 4). Data sent to them is governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Data processed on your device

The following data is stored only on your computer, in the App's local data folder. The Developer has no access to it:

  • Microphone audio: The App uses microphone permission only for voice commands and conversation. Your voice is converted to text by a speech recognition model on your device. Temporary audio created during processing is not stored permanently. Voice data is not used to identify you (no biometric recognition). Your voice leaves your device only in the live voice chat mode, and only if you turn that mode on (see Section 4).
  • Chat history and conversation memory: Recent conversations are kept on your device so the App can keep track of the conversation. Conversation memory is reset in 1-hour cycles; the written chat archive is deleted 5 days after the last message.
  • Conversation turn log: To measure response time and improve local command understanding, the text you say/type, Yumi's reply and timings are kept in daily files on your device. This log is never sent anywhere; entries older than 30 days are deleted automatically.
  • Settings and preferences: information you enter, such as character, voice, theme, city, microphone choice, phrases you teach the App, alarms and timers, and home or work address.
  • Account keys and sessions: API keys and sign-in data you enter for services such as Gemini, ElevenLabs, Spotify or calendars are stored on your device. Where possible, keys are encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) and tied to your Windows account. Gemini and ChatGPT web sessions are kept in a browser profile that the App keeps separate from your personal browser.
  • Smart home information: devices you add in the Home panel and devices found by scanning your local network (local IP address, device name, manufacturer).
  • Error logs: technical logs kept on your device for troubleshooting. They are never sent anywhere automatically.

You can remove this data from the App's settings or by deleting the App's data folder. When the Microsoft Store version of the App is uninstalled, Windows also deletes this data.

4. Third-party services used at your request

When you use the features below, the data they need is sent directly from your device to the respective provider. The Developer does not act as an intermediary and cannot see this content. These providers' servers may be located outside Türkiye, so connections you start may transfer your data abroad. Each provider handles the data under its own privacy policy and terms.

Services you use with your own account: The App connects to Gemini and ChatGPT through the browser on your device, using your own account; services that need an API key use your own key. For data sent to and stored by these services, the respective provider (e.g. Google, OpenAI) is the data controller; that relationship is between you and the provider. The Developer is neither a data controller nor a data processor for this data and cannot access it. You are responsible for the security of your account, the content you share and compliance with that service's terms.

  • Chat (web) (Google Gemini, OpenAI ChatGPT): Messages you type, or speak and have converted to text; you sign in with your own account
  • Chat (API) and live voice chat (Google, Gemini API): Your messages; in live voice chat mode, your microphone audio is streamed to Google in real time
  • Optional cloud voice (ElevenLabs): The reply text to be spoken
  • Music and video (YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify, radio streaming sites): Search terms; Spotify account authorization
  • Calendar (Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook): Your calendar events (if you grant access)
  • Weather (Open-Meteo): City name or coordinates
  • Currency, crypto and news (Frankfurter, Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye, CoinGecko, Google News, Wikipedia): The query (e.g. currency code); no personal data
  • Location and routes (ip-api.com, ipapi.co, OpenStreetMap Nominatim, OSRM): Approximate location from your IP address; the address you search for
  • Smart home (Tuya, Xiaomi and other device manufacturers): Device commands, for devices you set up with a cloud key
  • Clock check (Open-Meteo, Cloudflare): A request with no personal data (to verify the device clock)

Every internet connection necessarily reveals your IP address to the provider you connect to.

Key privacy policies:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/
  • ElevenLabs: https://elevenlabs.io/privacy-policy
  • Spotify: https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/
  • Microsoft: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

5. Microsoft Store

The App is downloaded, installed and updated through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft processes the related data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Microsoft gives the Developer only aggregated download statistics and technical health reports compiled from Windows error reports, which do not directly identify you. You can manage Windows error reporting in your Windows settings.

6. Data processed by the Developer

The Developer processes personal data only in these cases:

  • Contact: If you email us, your email address, your name and the content of your message.
  • Purpose: to answer your question, provide support, and handle requests made under KVKK.
  • Method of collection: your email reaching us (electronic means).
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)); for KVKK requests, compliance with a legal obligation (Art. 5(2)(ç)).
  • Retention: deleted within a reasonable time after the correspondence ends, and at the latest after 2 years.
  • Microsoft Store reports: the aggregated and technical reports described in Section 5. Purpose: improving the App's stability. Legal basis: legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).

This data is never sold, rented or used for advertising. It may be disclosed only to public authorities legally entitled to request it, where a legal obligation applies. The servers of the Developer's email provider (Google) may be located outside Türkiye.

7. Browser extension

The Yumi browser extension works only on YouTube pages, where it skips ads and manages tabs. It does not collect personal data and sends no data to any server. It communicates only with the Yumi app on your own computer, over a local connection (127.0.0.1).

8. Children

The App is not directed at children. The third-party services used for chat have their own age requirements (for example, Google and OpenAI services). We recommend that users under 18 use the App under the supervision of a parent or guardian.

9. Security

The App keeps your data on your device. Where possible, account keys are encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI). You are responsible for the security of your device and your Windows account.

10. Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you may contact the data controller to:

a) learn whether your personal data is processed;
b) request information about that processing;
c) learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used accordingly;
d) know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom your data is transferred;
e) request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data;
f) request erasure or destruction of your data under the conditions of Article 7 of KVKK;
g) request that third parties to whom the data was transferred be notified of actions taken under (e) and (f);
h) object to a result against you that arises from analysis carried out exclusively by automated systems;
i) claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

How to apply: Send your request in writing, or by email to aedogan.mail@gmail.com, with information that verifies your identity (full name, contact details and the subject of your request), in line with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. Your request will be answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If you find the answer unsatisfactory, or receive no answer in time, you may file a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Kurulu). If you live in another country, you may also have rights under your local law; you can use the same contact address.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated when new features are added or the law changes. The current version is always published on this page, with the date at the top updated. Significant changes will also be announced in the App.

In case of any discrepancy between language versions, the Turkish version prevails.
